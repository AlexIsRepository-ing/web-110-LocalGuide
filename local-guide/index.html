HILTON HEAD ISLAND

Hilton Head Island is a barrier island in South Carolina.

Its storied history and beautiful landscapes make it an amazing destination for family and friends.

Explore a Tropical Paradise

From golf courses, to sandy beaches, to cross-island bike trails, theres always something to discover for any kind of adventurer.

There's plenty of options for both solo travellers and families, as well as several shopping centers and community events to explore.

  • Shelter Cove
  • Golf Courses
  • Coligny Plaza

Get a Taste of the Lowcountry

Hilton Head Island's location on the east coast is the perfect part of the country to produce some of the freshest seafood dishes from lowcountry boil to fresh lobster.

Hilton Head is also home to the famous Salty Dog Cafe which has a few restaurants and merchandise stores scattered throughout the island. So chow down on some fried fish and hush puppies and take home a souvenir to remember your stay on the island!
